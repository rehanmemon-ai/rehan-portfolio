Engineering Principles

I prioritize software clarity, deterministic reproducibility, and evidence-grounded engineering over hype:

  • Deterministic Retrieval: When building RAG pipelines, I enforce explicit source citation mapping, cosine similarity thresholds, and chunk-level metadata tracking.
  • Defensive Persistence: Concurrency issues and race conditions must be resolved through content-addressed storage (SHA-256), atomic write protocols, and revision tokens.
  • Empirical Validation: I verify system capabilities through automated test suites (pytest, vitest) and objective benchmarks rather than anecdotal impressions.
